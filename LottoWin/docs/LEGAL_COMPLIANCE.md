# Legal & Responsible Gaming Notes

This software is provided as a template. You are solely responsible for:

- Compliance with gambling / lottery laws in every jurisdiction you operate.
- Age verification (18+ / 21+ as required).
- Obtaining any necessary licences.
- Anti-money-laundering (AML) and KYC procedures.
- Clear terms of service and privacy policy.
- Responsible gaming tools and self-exclusion.

The bot includes:

- Age warning (18+)
- Limit setting
- Time-out / self-exclusion
- Links to BeGambleAware

These are **minimum** tools. They do **not** replace legal advice or a full compliance programme.

Do not launch publicly without legal review.
